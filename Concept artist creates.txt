Concept:
artist creates account
artist agrees on policy and uploads art
admin reviews and accept 
Quirka notifies artist when his art is accepted
art is visible in shop
customer makes order 
Quirka send order to Printlet (via api integration)
Printlet prints and deliver to customer 
customers receives order
After 14 days of customer receiving order, Quirka sends money to artist account on Quirka
Artist chooses to receive his payment to his preferred bank account

Artist dashboard:
- track orders (filter by customer/date/art)
- graphical dashboard with filters
- artist has wallet and can track his transactions
- artist has option to transfer money from wallet to bank account
- artist has controller over his art to delete/edit/make invisible 

manager dashboard:
- track orders (filter by artist/customer/date/art)
- graphical dashboard with filters
- track artists wallets transactions
